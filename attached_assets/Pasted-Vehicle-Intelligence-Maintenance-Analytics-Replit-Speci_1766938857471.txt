Vehicle Intelligence & Maintenance Analytics (Replit Specification)

After all required vehicle information is entered, the system must automatically generate a future maintenance analysis for each vehicle.

Core Logic

Each vehicle’s maintenance forecast is driven by total kilometers traveled, calculated primarily from the assigned Sales Rep’s route activity.

The system must continuously aggregate:

Daily route KM

Weekly / Monthly cumulative KM

Total lifetime KM per vehicle

Rep–Vehicle Assignment Behavior

As soon as a Rep is assigned to a vehicle, the system must:

Link the Rep’s route data to that vehicle

Begin real-time KM accumulation

Trigger a full vehicle performance and condition analysis

Maintenance Analysis Engine

Based on total and projected KM, the system should:

Estimate wear & tear levels

Predict upcoming maintenance needs

Flag critical maintenance thresholds

Example outputs:

Next oil change (KM & estimated date)

Tire replacement window

Brake service forecast

General service intervals

Risk indicators for overdue maintenance

Predictive Forecasting

Use historical route data + future route scheduling to:

Predict KM growth rate

Estimate when each maintenance event will be due

Provide early warnings before thresholds are reached

Vehicle Dashboard (Mandatory)

Each vehicle must have its own dedicated dashboard, showing:

1. Vehicle Overview

Vehicle ID / Plate

Assigned Rep

Current odometer

Total KM (lifetime)

KM this month / quarter

2. Usage & Performance

Average KM per day

Route intensity (light / medium / heavy usage)

Comparison vs fleet average

3. Maintenance Status

Last maintenance date & KM

Upcoming maintenance items

Days / KM remaining to next service

Overdue alerts (if any)

4. System Recommendations

Clear maintenance recommendations

Priority level (Low / Medium / High)

Suggested maintenance timeline

5. Alerts & Notifications

Automatic alerts when:

Maintenance thresholds are approaching

Critical limits are exceeded

Usage deviates significantly from normal patterns

Output & UX Requirements

Dashboard must update automatically

Visual indicators (green / amber / red)

Exportable maintenance summary per vehicle

Scalable to support fleet-level analytics later
