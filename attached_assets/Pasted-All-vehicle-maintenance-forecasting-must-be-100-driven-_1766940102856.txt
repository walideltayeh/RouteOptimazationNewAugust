All vehicle maintenance forecasting must be 100% driven by kilometers traveled (KM).

KM is calculated from:

The Rep’s actual route distance

The number of working days

The Rep’s assigned schedule and zones

KM traveled is the primary and mandatory working variable for all vehicle health, performance, and maintenance predictions.

Rep Route–Based KM Calculation
Route KM Engine

For each assigned Rep, the system must:

Calculate daily route KM based on:

Planned routes (optimized routes)

Actual driven routes (if GPS is available)

Multiply daily route KM by:

Number of working days per week

Assigned visit frequency (VF1 / VF2 / VF4 if applicable)

KM Aggregation Levels

The system must maintain:

Daily KM

Weekly KM

Monthly KM

Annual KM

Lifetime KM per vehicle

Once a Rep is assigned to a vehicle:

All Rep route KM is automatically attributed to that vehicle

Reassignment resets attribution from the reassignment date onward (no historical overwrite)

Vehicle Maintenance Forecast Engine
Maintenance Logic (KM-Based)

Maintenance forecasting must be calculated using:

Current total KM

Average KM per working day

Projected future KM (based on schedule)

The system should dynamically predict:

Oil change intervals

Tire rotation & replacement

Brake service

Major servicing milestones

Risk of accelerated wear due to heavy usage

Predictive Timeline

For each maintenance item, the system must display:

Remaining KM

Estimated calendar date

Confidence level (based on route stability)
